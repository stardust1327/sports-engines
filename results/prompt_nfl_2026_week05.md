## How each criterion is measured

- **Trench dominance (25%):** pressure rate (sacks + QB hits per dropback), allowed
  by each offense against generated by the other defense; line yards per designed
  run (losses count 120%, yards 5-10 count half, beyond 10 count zero). ESPN's
  pass-block and run-block win rates come from player tracking that isn't public
  play by play, so these stand in for them.
- **Net efficiency (25%):** EPA per play, offense minus defense, garbage time removed.
- **Passing matchups (20%):** the starting QB's EPA per dropback when blitzed (5+
  rushers, FTN charting) and when not, against what the defense allows in each,
  weighted by how often that defense blitzes. Pressure-specific QB numbers aren't
  public, so blitz splits stand in for "under pressure".
- **Situational & rest (15%):** home field (+1.7 points) and rest (+0.15 points per
  extra day), measured from 2019 and 2021-2025 results. Time zones and travel
  distance were tested and showed no effect in those seasons (the estimates even
  leaned toward the travelling team), so they score zero. Weather changes the
  projected total, not the side (all outdoor winds 6-11 mph today). Look-ahead
  spots can't be measured, so they aren't scored.
- **High-leverage execution (15%):** third-down conversion rate, red-zone touchdown
  rate per trip, turnover margin per game.

Each criterion becomes a home-minus-away edge scaled so a typical game is 1,
weighted 25/25/20/15/15, and turned into a win chance, margin and total by a
week-by-week replay of 2022-2025. **Confidence = win chance in tenths** (7 = about
70%). The prompt's own inputs exclude betting lines; so does this.

**QB checks:** Jayden Daniels is reported starting for Washington (Schefter, Oct 8);
Jameis Winston starts for the Giants (Jaxson Dart out for the season). Games
whose regular starter is out or unconfirmed get no pick.
